# Toolkit icon sources

Technology marks in `toolkit.svg` and `toolkit-light.svg` are embedded from [Devicon](https://github.com/devicons/devicon/tree/6a37858047b0e8d9c9c0e68f4a071fc410652e89), pinned to commit `6a37858047b0e8d9c9c0e68f4a071fc410652e89`.

Devicon is distributed under the MIT License. The original copyright notice and complete license are retained in [devicon-LICENSE.txt](devicon-LICENSE.txt). Brand names and logos remain the property of their respective owners.

Layout, labels, and theme styling are custom. Next.js, Express, Django, MySQL, and AWS receive color adjustments in the dark version for legibility; all marks preserve their source geometry. The SVGs contain the vector paths directly and do not request external images, fonts, or scripts.
